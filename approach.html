My approach

Connect first. Then speak.

My Meisner training taught me one thing above all: connection comes first. From there, I learned how to truly listen, read behaviour and trust my instincts — skills that help me to meet each child where they are.

Inspired by Meisner, I bring games into the classroom that encourage eye contact and real listening in a playful way — life skills that help us connect with another person or an audience.

I use games and challenges to distract the children whilst they read aloud, shifting their attention onto the task rather than themselves.

That's the same principle behind Meisner's own words: "Acting is behaving truthfully under imaginary circumstances." Connection first, performance follows.

Read Aloud Club sits between and supports both voice class and drama class — a space to explore their voice with real technique and real practice, building the connection skills that carry into both.

I spent my teens and twenties struggling to use my voice. Read Aloud Club is the space I wish I'd had — built with real intention, so children can practise using theirs.
