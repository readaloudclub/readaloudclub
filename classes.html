Classes

Classes run in term time for home educators and in school holidays for everyone. You can join the full five-week course or try a single workshop first.

Read Aloud Club

AGES 4–6 · 45 MINS

Connection first, then play — building up to story, in their own voice.

  • Connection games to build trust
  • Vocal exercises to warm up their voice
  • Playful vocal games and fun tongue twisters
  • A story is read aloud and brought to life through play
£50 for the five-week course — includes a printed community challenge each week and a certificate for course completion.

Read Aloud Club

AGES 6–10 · 1 HOUR

Connection first, then technique and vocal games — building up to reading aloud together.

  • Connection game that builds trust
  • Vocal warm up and vocal technique
  • Articulation using fun tongue twisters
  • Games that encourage eye contact and real listening
  • Reading aloud together — as a group, in pairs and individually
£50 for the five-week course — includes a printed community challenge each week and a certificate for course completion.

Read Aloud Club

AGES 11–16 · 1 HOUR

The same foundations, pushed further — real connection, real technique and real reading aloud practice.

  • Connection game that builds trust
  • Vocal warm up and vocal technique
  • Deeper listening and eye contact exercise
  • Solo read aloud time with expressive delivery
£50 for the five-week course — includes a printed community challenge each week and a certificate for course completion.
