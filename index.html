Read Aloud Club is a unique space for children to explore their voice, learn real connection skills and practise reading aloud — all through play.

I'm Sarah, Meisner-trained founder, on a mission to help children connect with their voice, fearlessly.

The club

One class. Three ages.

Every class runs during term time and in the school holidays.

Read Aloud Club

AGES 4–6 · 45 MINS

Connection first, then play — building up to story, in their own voice.

Read Aloud Club

AGES 6–10 · 1 HOUR

Connection first, then technique and vocal games — building up to reading aloud together.

Read Aloud Club

AGES 11–16 · 1 HOUR

The same foundations, pushed further — real connection, real technique and real reading aloud practice.

Workshops

THEMED EVENTS

One-off sessions built around a story or a writer.

Beyond the classroom

Weekly community challenges.

Every week, I challenge children to read aloud with someone in their community, or connect with their own family. It builds relationships, connection and confidence — all at once.
